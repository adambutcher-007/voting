## Background & Record

Incumbent sheriff; elected in 2022 as the first Black person and first woman to lead the department ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/hennepin-county-dfl-sheriff-endorsement/601856965)).

## Party Service

The lone Democrat in the race, but withdrew from the DFL endorsement process after receiving 58% (short of the required 60%) at the county convention ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/hennepin-county-dfl-sheriff-endorsement/601856965)).

## Opposition & Criticism

Criticized over deputies' handling of protesters at the Whipple Federal Building during Operation Metro Surge, including recorded tackles and arrests of demonstrators ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/hennepin-county-dfl-sheriff-endorsement/601856965)).

## Endorsements

No DFL endorsement (see above). She has endorsed Anders Folk for county attorney ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/hennepin-county-dfl-sheriff-endorsement/601856965)).

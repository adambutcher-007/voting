# Hennepin County Sheriff, 2026

Incumbent Dawanna Witt filed for re-election; Joseph Banks, a 2022 challenger, filed again ([Hometown Source](https://www.hometownsource.com/sun_sailor/free/2-file-for-hennepin-county-sheriff/article_61502721-8be7-4b4a-9a16-2d48212c113a.html)). Witt failed to secure the county DFL endorsement amid disputes over her office's handling of federal immigration enforcement ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/hennepin-county-dfl-sheriff-endorsement/601856965)).

**Updated 2026-08-07:** Days before the Aug. 11 primary, Witt said federal officials threatened her with criminal witness-tampering charges over her office's non-cooperation with ICE ([MPR News](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/08/06/hennepin-county-sheriff-dawanna-witt-says-feds-threatened-to-arrest-her)) — see her candidate file for detail and differing coverage.

## Primary Results (Aug. 11, 2026)

No primary was held: only incumbent Dawanna Witt and challenger Joseph Banks filed for the single seat, so both proceed directly to the Nov. 3 general election under Minnesota's nonpartisan-primary rules ([hometownsource.com](https://www.hometownsource.com/psa/2-file-for-hennepin-county-sheriff/article_61502721-8be7-4b4a-9a16-2d48212c113a.html), accessed 2026-08-12).

## Platform

Platform centers on regular community "listening sessions," increased community-based programming, and ensuring deputies are visible and engaged, arguing that trust and safety grow when the Sheriff's Office connects with the people it serves ([banks4sheriff.com](https://www.banks4sheriff.com/)).

## Background & Record

More than 20 years in law enforcement. Started as a Police Explorer with the Boy Scouts of America in south suburban Illinois, then joined the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve before working as a police dispatcher and part-time officer; moved to Minnesota in 1993 ([Spokesman-Recorder](https://spokesman-recorder.com/2022/05/20/meet-hennepin-county-sheriff-candidate-joseph-banks/)). Became the Brainerd Police Department's first African American police officer intern while attending Central Lakes College; served in the Minnesota Army National Guard while completing a criminal justice degree ([Spokesman-Recorder](https://spokesman-recorder.com/2022/05/20/meet-hennepin-county-sheriff-candidate-joseph-banks/)). Served as chief of police for the Lower Sioux, Upper Sioux, and Morton, Minnesota police departments ([Spokesman-Recorder](https://spokesman-recorder.com/2022/05/20/meet-hennepin-county-sheriff-candidate-joseph-banks/)). CEO/president of Banks Securities Inc., which assists with criminal expungement resources; currently a licensed bail bondsman; co-founder of the Twin Cities Recovery Project ([Spokesman-Recorder](https://spokesman-recorder.com/2022/05/20/meet-hennepin-county-sheriff-candidate-joseph-banks/), [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Joseph_Banks)).

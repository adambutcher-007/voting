# Minnesota Attorney General, 2026

Incumbent Keith Ellison (DFL) is running for a third term; Ron Schutz is running in the Republican primary ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Minnesota_Attorney_General_election), [MN SoS certified candidates](https://www.sos.mn.gov/about-the-office/news-room/candidates-certified-for-2026-primary-election/)).

## Primary Results (Aug. 11, 2026)

DFL incumbent Keith Ellison won with 24,202 votes (81.22%) over David J.S. Madgett (5,596, 18.78%). Republican Ron Schutz was unopposed (22,232 votes, 100%). Ellison and Schutz advance to the Nov. 3 general election ([MN SOS official results](https://electionresults.sos.mn.gov/Results/Index?ersElectionId=200&scenario=AllwithoutJudicial&electionDate=8%2F11%2F2026%2012%3A00%3A00%20AM), accessed 2026-08-12).

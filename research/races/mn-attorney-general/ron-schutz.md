## Platform

Running on a platform of cracking down on violent crime, an "aggressive" and "innovative" approach to preventing and prosecuting fraud, and proposing to give the attorney general authority to take over criminal cases county attorneys decline to prosecute ([KTTC](https://www.kttc.com/2026/03/25/one-on-one-republican-minnesota-attorney-general-candidate-ron-schutz/)).

## Background & Record

Born and raised on a dairy farm near Adrian, Minnesota; attended Marquette University on an Army ROTC scholarship and earned a law degree from the University of Minnesota ([Ballotpedia, via search summary](https://ballotpedia.org/Ron_Schutz)). Served four years in the Army JAG Corps with the 7th Infantry Division, working as both a defense lawyer and a prosecutor ([search-aggregated bio](https://ballotpedia.org/Ron_Schutz)). Spent nearly 38 years in private practice at Robins Kaplan LLP, a roughly 200-lawyer litigation firm, serving as its chairman from 2019–2024, before leaving to run for office ([Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/03/top-twin-cities-trial-star-ditches-power-firm-to-take-on-keith-ellison/)). Named to Forbes' list of America's Top 200 lawyers ([KTTC](https://www.kttc.com/2026/03/25/one-on-one-republican-minnesota-attorney-general-candidate-ron-schutz/)).

# Eden Prairie Mayor, 2026

The mayor's seat is on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot; the term runs Jan. 1, 2027 through Dec. 31, 2030 ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). Filing closed at 5 p.m. July 28, 2026, and **incumbent Mayor Ron Case was the only candidate to file**, leaving the race uncontested as filed ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). Case is completing his second term as mayor after five and a half terms on the City Council ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)).

**Field is now final:** no candidates withdrew by the 5 p.m. July 30, 2026 deadline ([EP Local News](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). Case runs unopposed on the Nov. 3 ballot. The office is nonpartisan and there is no municipal primary ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/elections)).

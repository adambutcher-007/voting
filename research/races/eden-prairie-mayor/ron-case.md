## Background & Record

Case was the only candidate to file for mayor before the filing period closed at 5 p.m. July 28, 2026 ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). He is completing his second term as mayor after serving five and a half terms on the City Council ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)); he was re-elected mayor in the 2022 election and his current term ends Dec. 31, 2026 ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/ron-case)). Case taught in the Eden Prairie School District from 1979 through 2012, then worked at UnitedHealth Group from 2013 until retiring in April 2024 ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/ron-case)). He earned a doctorate in leadership from St. Mary's University of Minnesota in 2008, along with a master's degree in counseling psychology and a bachelor's degree in education, and is licensed in public school administration ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/ron-case)).

## Party Service

The mayor's office is nonpartisan. Case has served the City on commissions and task forces including the Heritage Preservation Commission, and has chaired the Suburban Rate Authority and the Southwest Suburban Cable Commission ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/ron-case)).

## News & Notes

**2026-09-05 general-election update:** Running unopposed for a third term as mayor; no other candidate filed, so Case is the only name that will appear for this office on the Nov. 3 ballot ([EP Local News](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/08/20/ron-case-unopposed-in-bid-for-third-term-as-eden-prairie-mayor/)). Has served 30 years on the City Council total, including eight as mayor.

The mayor's seat carries a term running Jan. 1, 2027 through Dec. 31, 2030 ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)).

# Minnesota State Auditor, 2026

Open seat: incumbent Julie Blaha (DFL) announced in September 2025 she is not seeking re-election ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/twin-cities/2025/09/04/minnesota-auditor-race-julie-blaha-2026), [Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/briefs/minnesota-state-auditor-julie-blaha-wont-seek-reelection-in-2026/)). DFL primary: Zack Filipovich, Adam Jennings, Ben Schierer, Dan Wolgamott. Republican primary: Elliott Engen, Nate George, Scott Jensen ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Minnesota_Auditor_election,_2026)). Jay Reeves is also listed for the general election ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Minnesota_Auditor_election,_2026)).

## Primary Results (Aug. 11, 2026)

DFL: Zack Filipovich won unopposed (26,525 votes, 100%) after Adam Jennings, Ben Schierer, and Dan Wolgamott dropped out following the DFL endorsement ([Valley News Live](https://www.valleynewslive.com/2026/08/12/zack-filipovich-wins-dfl-primary-minnesota-state-auditor/), accessed 2026-08-12). Republican: Scott Jensen won with 15,754 votes (66.84%), defeating GOP-endorsed Nate George (6,158, 26.13%) and Will Finn (1,656, 7.03%); Elliott Engen had already withdrawn to seek re-election to his MN House seat after a DWI arrest ([KSTP](https://kstp.com/kstp-news/top-news/elliott-engen-drops-bid-for-auditor-files-for-new-term-in-minnesota-house-after-dwi-arrest/), accessed 2026-08-12). Filipovich and Jensen advance to the Nov. 3 general election ([MN SOS official results](https://electionresults.sos.mn.gov/Results/Index?ersElectionId=200&scenario=AllwithoutJudicial&electionDate=8%2F11%2F2026%2012%3A00%3A00%20AM), accessed 2026-08-12).

## Platform

Running on accountability and fiscal responsibility: pledges to earn trust through consistent oversight of how local governments spend tax dollars, and to proactively follow up to ensure prosecutors actually pursue fraud cases once found ([nategeorgeformnstateauditor.com](https://nategeorgeformnstateauditor.com/)).

## Background & Record

Air Force veteran; mayor of Braham, Minnesota; director of licensing and investigations for the Mille Lacs Band of Ojibwe's Gaming Regulatory Authority, bringing professional experience in investigations, licensing, compliance, and oversight ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Nate_George)).

## Party Service

Received the Republican Party of Minnesota's endorsement for State Auditor ([MN GOP](https://mngop.com/republican-party-of-minnesota-congratulates-nate-george-on-earning-gop-endorsement-for-state-auditor-2/)).

## Opposition & Criticism

MinnPost reported that George's campaign claimed on social media he was a "certified fraud examiner," a credential he does not actually hold ([MinnPost](https://www.minnpost.com/elections/2026/07/nate-george-state-auditor-resume-claims-certified-fraud-examiner/)). Following the report, George's campaign clarified posts that had misstated his qualifications ([KSTP](https://kstp.com/kstp-news/local-news/gop-endorsed-candidate-for-state-auditor-clarifies-posts-that-misstated-qualifications/)).

## Platform

Says his experience working in the audit section of a bank and his time chairing committees in the Minnesota Senate qualify him for the auditor role ([Yahoo News, via search summary](https://ballotpedia.org/Scott_Jensen_(Minnesota))).

## Background & Record

Physician; earned an undergraduate degree and a medical degree from the University of Minnesota; owner of Catalyst Medical Clinic PA ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Scott_Jensen_(Minnesota))). Member of the Minnesota State Senate representing District 47 from January 2017 to January 5, 2021 ([MN Legislative Reference Library](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/legdb/fulldetail?id=15484)). Republican nominee for Minnesota governor in 2022 (lost to incumbent DFL Gov. Tim Walz). On February 9, 2026, withdrew from the 2026 Minnesota governor's race and announced a run for State Auditor instead ([KTTC](https://www.kttc.com/2026/02/09/scott-jensen-withdraws-minnesota-governors-race-pursue-state-auditor-position/), [MPR News](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/02/09/jensen-drops-out-gop-race-minnesota-governor-run-state-auditor)).

## Platform

Originally campaigned on expanding the State Auditor's office and increasing government accountability ([Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/rep-engen-drops-financial-watchdog-161407000.html)). Dropped the auditor bid before the June 2, 2026 filing deadline and instead filed for re-election to his Minnesota House seat ([Fox 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/rep-engen-drops-financial-watchdog-state-auditor-bid-files-mn-house-reelection-june-2026)).

## Background & Record

Born October 6, 1998 in St. Paul, MN; lives in White Bear Township. Earned a B.A. from Hamline University. First Generation Z Republican elected to the Minnesota Legislature, representing House District 36A (Centerville, Circle Pines, Lino Lakes in Anoka County; North Oaks and parts of White Bear Township in Ramsey County) since January 3, 2023 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Elliott_Engen)). Serves as an assistant minority leader for the House Republican caucus; sits on the Human Services Policy, Public Safety Finance and Policy, and Rules and Legislative Administration Committees ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Elliott_Engen)).

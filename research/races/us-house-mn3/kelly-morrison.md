## Background & Record

Incumbent US Representative for MN-3, first elected 2024; physician (OB-GYN); former Minnesota state senator and state representative ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Kelly_Morrison), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Morrison)).

## Party Service

Held a suburban state legislative seat for the DFL before winning MN-3, keeping the seat in DFL hands after Dean Phillips' departure ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Morrison)).

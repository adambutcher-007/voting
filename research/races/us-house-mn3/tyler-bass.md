## Platform

Says he wants to bring "real-world experience, common sense leadership, and accountability back to Washington" ([bassforcongress.com](https://bassforcongress.com/)). On jobs and the economy, believes Minnesota must do more to support American workers and small businesses, favors holding large corporations accountable, prioritizing U.S. citizens in hiring, and making it impossible for employers to hire illegal labor ([bassforcongress.com/about](https://bassforcongress.com/about/)).

## Background & Record

High school diploma from Blooming Prairie High School; bachelor's degree from Luther College (2010) ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Tyler_Bass)). Founder of At Home Fitness, an in-home personal training company; later expanded into construction, working as a general contractor on interior and exterior projects across the Twin Cities ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Tyler_Bass)). Small business owner living in Minnetonka, Minnesota, with over 12 years of business-ownership experience ([bassforcongress.com/about](https://bassforcongress.com/about/)).

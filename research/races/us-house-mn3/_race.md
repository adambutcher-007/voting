# US House — Minnesota's 3rd District, 2026

Eden Prairie is in MN-3. Incumbent Kelly Morrison (DFL) is running in the Democratic primary; Tyler Bass and Jeremy Westby are running in the Republican primary ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Minnesota's_3rd_Congressional_District_election,_2026)).

## Primary Results (Aug. 11, 2026)

Republican: Tyler Bass won with 22,589 votes (55.43%), defeating Quentin Wittrock (18,165, 44.57%); Jeremy Westby was not on the final primary ballot. DFL incumbent Kelly Morrison was unopposed (86,218 votes, 100%). Bass and Morrison advance to the Nov. 3 general election ([MN SOS official results](https://electionresults.sos.mn.gov/Results/Index?ersElectionId=200&scenario=USRepresentative&districtId=558), accessed 2026-08-12).

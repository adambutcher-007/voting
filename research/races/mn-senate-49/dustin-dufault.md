## Background & Record

Minnetonka patent attorney ([EP Local News](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/05/28/gop-endorses-candidates-for-eden-prairie-area-legislative-races/)). Grew up on a farm in the Red River Valley in northwest Minnesota; earned a B.S. in Chemical Engineering before attending William Mitchell College of Law ([dufaultformn.com](https://dufaultformn.com/)). Founder of DuFault Law Firm, P.C., practicing patent protection, trademark dispute litigation, and business litigation; licensed to practice before the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office ([dufault-law.com](https://www.dufault-law.com/about)). Has lived in Minnetonka for 16 years with his wife Carolina ([dufaultformn.com](https://dufaultformn.com/)).

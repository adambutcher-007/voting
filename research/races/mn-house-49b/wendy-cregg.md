## Platform

Campaign centers on "restoring accountability" in Minnesota government, drawing on her background combating insurance fraud ([EP Local News reader letter](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/06/23/cregg-would-restore-accountability-in-minnesota/)).

## Background & Record

Eden Prairie resident and former nurse practitioner ([EP Local News](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/05/28/gop-endorses-candidates-for-eden-prairie-area-legislative-races/)). Graduated summa cum laude from the University of Wisconsin–Eau Claire in 1980 with a B.S. in Nursing, and earned a Master of Science in Nursing from Minnesota State University, Mankato in 2012, becoming a certified Family Nurse Practitioner ([hometownsource.com](https://www.hometownsource.com/sun_sailor/free/eden-prairies-cregg-announces-republican-bid-for-hd-49b/article_d79bb7c1-a9af-41b9-b4b4-ca9ed3fd42b4.html)). Worked seven years as a fraud investigator at Optum, part of UnitedHealth Group, focusing on large and complex fraud cases, and was nominated for the UHG Sages of Clinical Service Award ([hometownsource.com](https://www.hometownsource.com/sun_sailor/free/eden-prairies-cregg-announces-republican-bid-for-hd-49b/article_d79bb7c1-a9af-41b9-b4b4-ca9ed3fd42b4.html)). Was the first woman to win Grandma's Marathon, according to a reader letter to the editor ([EP Local News reader letter](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/06/23/cregg-would-restore-accountability-in-minnesota/) — unverified against a second source).

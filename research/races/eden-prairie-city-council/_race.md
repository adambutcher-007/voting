# Eden Prairie City Council, 2026

Two at-large seats are on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot, with terms running Jan. 1, 2027 through Dec. 31, 2030 ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). **Seven candidates filed** before the 5 p.m. July 28, 2026 deadline: incumbents Mark Freiberg and Kathy Nelson, plus Tony Arndt, Brad Canham, Tina Culbertson, Greg Lehman and Dirk Tedmon ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)).

Freiberg is completing his second term on the council and Nelson her fifth ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). Lehman was previously on the ballot for the council in 2022 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Greg_Lehman_(Eden_Prairie_City_Council,_Minnesota,_candidate_2022))), and Tedmon sought the DFL endorsement in state Senate District 49 earlier in 2026 ([DFL SD49](https://dfl49.org/2026-candidates-seeking-endorsement)).

**Field is now final:** no candidates withdrew by the 5 p.m. July 30, 2026 deadline ([EP Local News](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). The race is nonpartisan and there is no municipal primary ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/elections)); all seven names appear on the November ballot as filed.

## Background & Record

Lehman filed for one of the two at-large council seats ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). He previously ran for the Eden Prairie City Council and was on the ballot in the Nov. 8, 2022 general election ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Greg_Lehman_(Eden_Prairie_City_Council,_Minnesota,_candidate_2022))). In his 2022 Ballotpedia survey he described a 30-year career in technical support and 21 years working part time at a children's hospital ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Greg_Lehman_(Eden_Prairie_City_Council,_Minnesota,_candidate_2022))).

## Party Service

City council races are nonpartisan.

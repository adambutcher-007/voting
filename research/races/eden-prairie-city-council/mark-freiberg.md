## Background & Record

Freiberg filed for reelection to one of the two at-large council seats ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). He is completing his second term on the council ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)); he was re-elected in 2022 and his current term ends Dec. 31, 2026 ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/mark-freiberg)). He spent 30 years in the real estate and housing finance industries and attended Normandale Community College ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/mark-freiberg)). He and his family have lived in Eden Prairie since 1981 ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/mark-freiberg)).

## Party Service

City council races are nonpartisan. Freiberg served on the City's Heritage Preservation Commission from 2013 to 2016 and the Planning Commission from 2016 to 2018, and his community involvement has included the Eden Prairie Community Foundation, the Historical Society and the Rotary Club ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/mark-freiberg)).

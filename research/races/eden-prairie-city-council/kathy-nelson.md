## Background & Record

Nelson filed for reelection to one of the two at-large council seats ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). She is completing her fifth term on the council ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)); she was re-elected to a fifth term in 2022 and her current term ends Dec. 31, 2026 ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/kathy-nelson)). Before her election to the council she was a longtime member of the City's Planning Commission ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/kathy-nelson)). She earned a bachelor's degree in communications from the University of Minnesota, and she and her family have lived in Eden Prairie since 1992 ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/kathy-nelson)).

## Party Service

City council races are nonpartisan. Nelson has served on the Southwest Light Rail Transit Policy Board and the Eden Prairie Community Foundation Board of Directors, currently represents the City on the Eden Prairie Fire Relief Association, and is a member of the League of Women Voters ([City of Eden Prairie](https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/city-government/city-council/kathy-nelson)).

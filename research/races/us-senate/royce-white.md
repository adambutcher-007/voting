## Background & Record

Former NBA player, drafted 16th overall by the Houston Rockets in 2012; also played for the Sacramento Kings, in the NBA G League, and for the London Lightning of Canada's NBL, winning two league championships and a 2017 MVP award ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Royce_White)). Was the 2024 Republican nominee for US Senate in Minnesota, losing to incumbent Amy Klobuchar by roughly 16 points ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Royce_White), [Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/adam-schwarze-wins-republican-gop-minnesota-senate-endorsement-michele-tafoya-primary/601845122)).

## Party Service

Sought the 2026 GOP endorsement; eliminated during the six-round convention balloting won by Schwarze ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/adam-schwarze-wins-republican-gop-minnesota-senate-endorsement-michele-tafoya-primary/601845122)). Remains in the August primary ([KARE 11](https://www.kare11.com/article/news/politics/minnesota-dfl-primary-for-us-senate-heats-up-between-lt-gov-peggy-flanagan-and-rep-angie-craig/89-5fde0e39-7ce5-42d0-b665-ff1e08f73bcf)).

## Campaign Finance

His 2022 US House campaign submitted amended finance reports labeling more than three dozen expenditures — including retail purchases and an alleged $1,200 reimbursement to a Miami strip club — as "non-authorized," claiming personal reimbursement; reporting found more than $157,000 in donor funds had gone to personal expenses including strip clubs, resorts, and clothing ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/royce-white-campaign-claims-to-reimburse-non-authorized-charges-on-strip-club-retail-water-parks/600381315)). Total career federal campaign spending across 2022, 2024, and the 2026 bid exceeds $2.4 million raised against $2.3 million spent ([Ballotpedia, citing FEC/OpenSecrets](https://ballotpedia.org/Royce_White)). See [FEC data](https://www.fec.gov/data/elections/senate/MN/2026/).

### Companies & Management Roles

### Political Offices Held
- White has never held elected office.
- **2022 (Feb–Aug):** Ran for the Republican nomination for US House, Minnesota's 5th Congressional District; lost the primary to Cicely Davis, 48.0% to 37.2% ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Royce_White)).
- **2024 (Aug–Nov):** Won the Republican primary for US Senate, then lost the general election to incumbent Sen. Amy Klobuchar (D), roughly 56%–40% ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Royce_White)).
- **2026:** Declared candidacy again for US Senate; running in the Aug. 11, 2026 Republican primary against Adam Schwarze (GOP-endorsed) and Michele Tafoya ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Royce_White), [Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/mn-senate-republican-gop-candidates-tuesday-primary-election-tafoya-schwarze-white/601874785)).

### Petitions
- **2016–2018:** Publicly campaigned via open letters and media appearances urging the NBA and National Basketball Players Association (NBPA) to adopt a formal mental-health policy for players; four doctors co-signed his open letter to the league ([Good Men Project](https://goodmenproject.com/featured-content/an-open-letter-to-the-nba-a-call-to-action-on-mental-health-mkdn/)).
- **2018-05 (May 2018):** Successful outcome — the NBPA hired its first mental health director, with the hire crediting White (among other players) as instrumental in prompting the change ([NBC News, cited via search](https://www.nbcnews.com/health/mental-health/how-two-nba-players-larry-sanders-royce-white-are-bringing-n1004351)).

### COVID-19 Stance
- **2024 (campaign platform):** Called COVID-19 mandates a "globalist COVID-19 power grab attempt to snatch away freedom" and pledged to "hold those responsible for the COVID-19 deceit accountable"; described official case/death reporting and media coverage as part of a broader "deceit" requiring accountability ([Royce White campaign site, via Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Royce_White)).
- *No information found* of an earlier, different COVID position or any stated reversal — his rhetoric on mandates appears consistent since 2022–2024.

### 2nd Amendment / Gun Policy
- **2026 (campaign site):** "The 2ND amendment is a constitutional right that can never be infringed upon. It is one of the most important political mechanisms to safeguard the freedoms of citizens in this country." ([roycewhite.us/issues](https://roycewhite.us/issues/)).

### Trump Alignment
- **2024:** Campaign website stated his top priorities "can be addressed on day 1 of Donald Trump's presidency," aligning his platform explicitly with a Trump administration ([Ballotpedia, quoting campaign site](https://ballotpedia.org/Royce_White)).
- **2026:** Received an endorsement from Trump advisor Steve Bannon during his 2026 Senate run ([The Week](https://theweek.com/politics/royce-white-amy-klobuchar-minnesota-jews-women-republicans)).
- **2026:** Has called for Trump to serve an unconstitutional third term ([The Week](https://theweek.com/politics/royce-white-amy-klobuchar-minnesota-jews-women-republicans); [Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/mn-senate-republican-gop-candidates-tuesday-primary-election-tafoya-schwarze-white/601874785)).

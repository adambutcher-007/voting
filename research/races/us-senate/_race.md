# US Senate — Minnesota, 2026

Open seat: incumbent Sen. Tina Smith (DFL) is retiring ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/United_States_Senate_election_in_Minnesota,_2026)). The DFL primary features Rep. Angie Craig, Lt. Gov. Peggy Flanagan (DFL-endorsed), and Billy Nord; the Republican primary features Adam Schwarze (GOP-endorsed), Michele Tafoya, and Royce White ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/United_States_Senate_election_in_Minnesota,_2026_(August_11_Democratic_primary)), [MinnPost](https://www.minnpost.com/elections/2026/05/gop-primary-awaits-after-schwarze-tops-tafoya-for-partys-u-s-senate-endorsement/)). The Flanagan–Craig contest has drawn record outside spending and is described as the most expensive primary in Minnesota history ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/angie-craig-peggy-flanagan-minnesota-senate-race-outside-spending-donor-pac/601851815)).

**Updated 2026-08-07:** With five days to go, polling on the DFL primary diverges by who's sponsoring it. A Flanagan-aligned pollster (DLGA-commissioned PPP text poll, July 23–24) shows her lead over Craig widening to 14 points (46%–32%), while earlier nonpartisan KSTP/SurveyUSA polling in June–July had shown Craig with a modest lead; outside spending remains lopsided (~$11.9M pro-Craig "North Star Dawn" vs. ~$2.4M DLGA pro-Flanagan) ([Minnesota Reformer, "State of the race for U.S. Senate," July 31](https://minnesotareformer.com/2026/07/31/state-of-the-race-for-u-s-senate/); [Native News Online, citing DLGA poll](https://nativenewsonline.net/currents/new-poll-shows-peggy-flanagan-expanding-lead-in-minnesota-democratic-u-s-senate-primary/)). Treat the DLGA-sponsored poll as a partisan source.

## Primary Results (Aug. 11, 2026)

DFL: Peggy Flanagan won with 17,425 votes (56.95%), defeating Rep. Angie Craig (12,654, 41.36%) and Billy Nord (130, 0.42%). Republican: Michele Tafoya won with 13,885 votes (55.82%), defeating GOP-endorsed Adam Schwarze (5,674, 22.81%) and Royce White (2,620, 10.53%). Flanagan and Tafoya advance to the Nov. 3 general election ([MN SOS official results](https://electionresults.sos.mn.gov/Results/Index?ersElectionId=200&scenario=AllwithoutJudicial&electionDate=8%2F11%2F2026%2012%3A00%3A00%20AM), accessed 2026-08-12).

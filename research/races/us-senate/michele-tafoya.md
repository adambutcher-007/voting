## News & Notes

**2026-09-05 general-election update:** Won the Aug. 11 Republican primary with 52.1% of the vote over Adam Schwarze and Royce White ([MPR News](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/08/11/michele-tafoya-wins-republican-nomination-for-senate)). Advances to face DFL nominee Peggy Flanagan on Nov. 3. Says Minnesotans feel "embarrassed" by recent progressive primary wins and is campaigning on government accountability, fraud, inflation, crime, and border security, aligned with President Trump ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/6028783-michele-tafoya-minnesota-senate/)). Republicans haven't won a statewide Minnesota race since 2006; Tafoya is aiming to attract independents.

A May 2026 Quantus Insights poll of likely GOP primary voters put her at 52%, roughly 43 points ahead of her nearest rival ([WLT Report — partisan outlet](https://wltreport.com/2026/05/24/michele-tafoya-opens-commanding-lead-in-minnesota-gop-senate-primary/)).

**2026-08-12:** Won the Aug. 11 Republican primary for US Senate with 13,885 votes (55.82%), advancing to face DFL nominee Peggy Flanagan on Nov. 3 ([MN SOS official results](https://electionresults.sos.mn.gov/Results/Index?ersElectionId=200&scenario=AllwithoutJudicial&electionDate=8%2F11%2F2026%2012%3A00%3A00%20AM), accessed 2026-08-12).

### 2nd Amendment / Gun Policy
- **Undated (2026 primary coverage):** Has expressed support for "red flag" laws — allowing law enforcement to temporarily remove weapons from individuals a court has assessed as a danger to themselves or others — a position opposed by many gun-rights convention delegates ([MinnPost](https://www.minnpost.com/national/washington/2026/05/michele-tafoya-has-support-of-national-gop-but-winning-over-state-activists-challenge/)).
- **Undated:** On a radio show, did not rule out supporting some type of gun limits, though her campaign said she wants to enforce existing laws rather than pass new ones ([MinnPost](https://www.minnpost.com/national/washington/2026/05/michele-tafoya-has-support-of-national-gop-but-winning-over-state-activists-challenge/)). A letter to the editor in a southern Minnesota paper separately argued she is "strong on 2nd Amendment," reflecting divided characterizations of her record.
- Analysis: this record of nuance is described as making her "the most moderate candidate in the Republican field," which drew backlash from some conservative convention delegates ([MinnPost](https://www.minnpost.com/national/washington/2026/05/michele-tafoya-has-support-of-national-gop-but-winning-over-state-activists-challenge/)).

### Trump Alignment
- **2022:** Wrote an open letter to Trump saying she hoped he would not run again for president, while praising his accomplishments and calling his politics "messy" ([citing search-aggregated reporting on her record]).
- **2026 (Senate campaign):** Has since aligned more closely with Trump's platform; said she would be "honored" to have his endorsement in the Senate race, adding, "if he wants to support or endorse, I would welcome it," while emphasizing she is running her own campaign ([NOTUS](https://www.notus.org/2026-election/republican-top-senate-recruit-minnesota-michele-tafoya-interview)).

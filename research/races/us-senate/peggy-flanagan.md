## Platform

Flanagan runs as a staunch progressive; she has campaigned against federal ICE enforcement tactics and against outside corporate money in the primary ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/minnesota-dfl-u-s-senate-primary-angie-craig-peggy-flanagan/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/twin-cities/2026/03/30/minnesota-senate-primary-flanagan-craig-ice-raids-dfl-endorsement-delegates)).

## Background & Record

Lieutenant Governor of Minnesota under Tim Walz ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/United_States_Senate_election_in_Minnesota,_2026)). Previously chaired the Democratic Lieutenant Governors Association (DLGA) ([KAAL/Craig campaign statement](https://www.kaaltv.com/news/lieutenant-governor-and-u-s-senate-candidate-peggy-flanagan-raises-questions-about-campaign-spending-rep-angie-craig-responds/)).

## Party Service

Won the 2026 Minnesota DFL endorsement for US Senate ([KARE 11](https://www.kare11.com/article/news/politics/minnesota-dfl-primary-for-us-senate-heats-up-between-lt-gov-peggy-flanagan-and-rep-angie-craig/89-5fde0e39-7ce5-42d0-b665-ff1e08f73bcf)); two terms as DFL lieutenant governor; chaired the DLGA ([KAAL](https://www.kaaltv.com/news/lieutenant-governor-and-u-s-senate-candidate-peggy-flanagan-raises-questions-about-campaign-spending-rep-angie-craig-responds/)).

## Opposition & Criticism

The Craig campaign says Flanagan "accepted millions of corporate dollars as chair of the DLGA — including from the pharma, tobacco, oil and gas industries and from an ICE detention center contractor" and misleads voters about it ([Craig campaign, via KAAL](https://www.kaaltv.com/news/lieutenant-governor-and-u-s-senate-candidate-peggy-flanagan-raises-questions-about-campaign-spending-rep-angie-craig-responds/)). Critics also press her over the state's Medicaid/fraud scandals that occurred during the Walz administration ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/minnesota-dfl-u-s-senate-primary-angie-craig-peggy-flanagan/)).

## Endorsements

Minnesota DFL endorsement ([KARE 11](https://www.kare11.com/article/news/politics/minnesota-dfl-primary-for-us-senate-heats-up-between-lt-gov-peggy-flanagan-and-rep-angie-craig/89-5fde0e39-7ce5-42d0-b665-ff1e08f73bcf)).

Claims $12M in outside PAC spending supports Craig, calling it the most expensive primary in state history ([Grand Forks Herald](https://www.grandforksherald.com/news/minnesota/flanagan-craig-clash-over-outside-spending-in-dfl-u-s-senate-primary)). ## News & Notes

**2026-09-05 general-election update:** Won the Aug. 11 DFL primary with 59% of the vote over Rep. Angie Craig ([Fox 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/minnesota-senate-primary-election-results-dfl-2026), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/11/nx-s1-5927473/2026-minnesota-senate-primary-election-peggy-flanagan)). Advances to face Republican nominee Michele Tafoya on Nov. 3. Campaigned against corporate influence and Trump-administration policy, with endorsements from Sen. Bernie Sanders and Sen. Elizabeth Warren ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/11/nx-s1-5927473/2026-minnesota-senate-primary-election-peggy-flanagan)).

ICE-enforcement backlash reportedly boosted her primary standing ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/twin-cities/2026/03/30/minnesota-senate-primary-flanagan-craig-ice-raids-dfl-endorsement-delegates)). Debated Craig on C-SPAN ([C-SPAN](https://www.c-span.org/program/campaign-2026/minnesota-us-senate-primary-debate-between-lt-gov-peggy-flanagan-and-rep-angie-craig/681707)).

**Updated 2026-08-07:** A DLGA-commissioned (Flanagan-aligned) poll taken July 23–24 shows her lead over Craig widening to 14 points (46%–32%), roughly double a prior 7-point lead; treat this as a partisan-sponsored poll, since earlier nonpartisan KSTP/SurveyUSA polling had shown Craig modestly ahead ([Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/2026/07/31/state-of-the-race-for-u-s-senate/), [Native News Online](https://nativenewsonline.net/currents/new-poll-shows-peggy-flanagan-expanding-lead-in-minnesota-democratic-u-s-senate-primary/)).

**2026-08-12:** Won the Aug. 11 DFL primary for US Senate with 17,425 votes (56.95%) over Angie Craig, advancing to face Republican nominee Michele Tafoya on Nov. 3 ([MN SOS official results](https://electionresults.sos.mn.gov/Results/Index?ersElectionId=200&scenario=AllwithoutJudicial&electionDate=8%2F11%2F2026%2012%3A00%3A00%20AM), accessed 2026-08-12).

## Platform

Craig runs as a pragmatic moderate, emphasizing her record of winning competitive districts and her work on agriculture policy as ranking member of the House Agriculture Committee ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/minnesota-dfl-u-s-senate-primary-angie-craig-peggy-flanagan/), [KARE 11](https://www.kare11.com/article/news/politics/minnesota-dfl-primary-for-us-senate-heats-up-between-lt-gov-peggy-flanagan-and-rep-angie-craig/89-5fde0e39-7ce5-42d0-b665-ff1e08f73bcf)).

## Background & Record

US Representative for Minnesota's 2nd District; flipped a Republican-held district in 2018 and is ranking member of the House Committee on Agriculture ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/minnesota-dfl-u-s-senate-primary-angie-craig-peggy-flanagan/), [19th News](https://19thnews.org/2025/07/minnesota-senate-primary-2026/)). Voted for the Laken Riley Act, a vote she now says she regrets ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/twin-cities/2026/03/30/minnesota-senate-primary-flanagan-craig-ice-raids-dfl-endorsement-delegates)).

## Party Service

Four-term DFL congresswoman who repeatedly held a swing district for the party ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/minnesota-dfl-u-s-senate-primary-angie-craig-peggy-flanagan/)). Sought but did not win the 2026 DFL endorsement, which went to Flanagan ([KARE 11](https://www.kare11.com/article/news/politics/minnesota-dfl-primary-for-us-senate-heats-up-between-lt-gov-peggy-flanagan-and-rep-angie-craig/89-5fde0e39-7ce5-42d0-b665-ff1e08f73bcf)).

## Opposition & Criticism

Flanagan attacks Craig for being the only Minnesota Democrat to vote for the Laken Riley Act ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/twin-cities/2026/03/30/minnesota-senate-primary-flanagan-craig-ice-raids-dfl-endorsement-delegates)); a Democratic outside group ran ads attacking her on ICE-related votes ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/democratic-group-launches-ice-attack-rep-angie-craig-minnesota-senate-rcna345888)). Flanagan also criticizes the ~$12 million in outside PAC spending supporting Craig ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/angie-craig-peggy-flanagan-minnesota-senate-race-outside-spending-donor-pac/601851815), [Grand Forks Herald](https://www.grandforksherald.com/news/minnesota/flanagan-craig-clash-over-outside-spending-in-dfl-u-s-senate-primary)).

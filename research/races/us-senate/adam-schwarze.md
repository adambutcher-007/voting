## Platform

Pitches himself as an uncompromising conservative closely aligned with President Trump ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/adam-schwarze-wins-republican-gop-minnesota-senate-endorsement-michele-tafoya-primary/601845122)).

## Background & Record

Former Navy SEAL, 42, born in St. Paul, raised in Bloomington; joined the Marines after 9/11, served in Iraq, guarded US embassies, then joined the SEALs with nine deployments in 70 countries ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/adam-schwarze-wins-republican-gop-minnesota-senate-endorsement-michele-tafoya-primary/601845122)). Says he entered politics after being arrested in 2020 for allegedly violating Hawaii's COVID quarantine rules ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/adam-schwarze-wins-republican-gop-minnesota-senate-endorsement-michele-tafoya-primary/601845122)).

## Party Service

Won the Minnesota GOP endorsement with ~63% of delegates after six rounds of balloting at the Duluth convention ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/adam-schwarze-wins-republican-gop-minnesota-senate-endorsement-michele-tafoya-primary/601845122), [MinnPost](https://www.minnpost.com/elections/2026/05/gop-primary-awaits-after-schwarze-tops-tafoya-for-partys-u-s-senate-endorsement/)).

## Opposition & Criticism

Rival Michele Tafoya argues her appeal to moderates and independents makes her the stronger statewide candidate versus his hardline positioning ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/adam-schwarze-wins-republican-gop-minnesota-senate-endorsement-michele-tafoya-primary/601845122)). A May Quantus Insights poll showed him trailing Tafoya by a wide margin in the primary ([WLT Report — partisan outlet](https://wltreport.com/2026/05/24/michele-tafoya-opens-commanding-lead-in-minnesota-gop-senate-primary/)).

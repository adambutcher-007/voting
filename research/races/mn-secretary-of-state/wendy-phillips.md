## Platform

Says she is running because "there is so little trust in our current election system, and I'd like to rebuild that trust" ([KIMT](https://www.kimt.com/news/wendy-phillips-promises-fresh-ideas-in-minnesota-secretary-of-state-race/article_903cfc75-1711-4a4a-be81-181cbd1d6495.html)). Platform focuses on voter identification, cleaning the voter rolls, and targeting businesses that exploit taxpayers ([KIMT](https://www.kimt.com/news/wendy-phillips-promises-fresh-ideas-in-minnesota-secretary-of-state-race/article_903cfc75-1711-4a4a-be81-181cbd1d6495.html)). Wants to eliminate electronics from the state's election system in favor of watermarked paper ballots and hand counting, return to one-day elections, and end mail-in voting with few exceptions ([KIMT](https://www.kimt.com/news/wendy-phillips-promises-fresh-ideas-in-minnesota-secretary-of-state-race/article_903cfc75-1711-4a4a-be81-181cbd1d6495.html)).

## Background & Record

Resident of Oronoco, Minnesota; moved to Minnesota from California six years ago. Longtime business owner and grassroots advocate; former Olmsted County GOP chair ([KIMT](https://www.kimt.com/news/wendy-phillips-promises-fresh-ideas-in-minnesota-secretary-of-state-race/article_903cfc75-1711-4a4a-be81-181cbd1d6495.html)). Serves on her church board; also served as Oronoco zoning and planning commissioner, MNGOP State Executive Board member, and chair of the Olmsted County BPOU ([KIMT](https://www.kimt.com/news/wendy-phillips-promises-fresh-ideas-in-minnesota-secretary-of-state-race/article_903cfc75-1711-4a4a-be81-181cbd1d6495.html)). Previously ran as the Republican Party's endorsed candidate for a State House seat in the Rochester suburbs ([Ballotpedia, via search summary](https://ballotpedia.org/Wendy_Phillips)).

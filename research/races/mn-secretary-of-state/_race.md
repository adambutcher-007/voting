# Minnesota Secretary of State, 2026

Incumbent Steve Simon (DFL) is running in the Democratic primary; Tad Jude and Wendy Phillips are running in the Republican primary ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Minnesota_Secretary_of_State_election,_2026), [MN SoS certified candidates](https://www.sos.mn.gov/about-the-office/news-room/candidates-certified-for-2026-primary-election/)).

## Primary Results (Aug. 11, 2026)

Republican: Tad Jude won with 15,176 votes (64.92%), defeating Wendy Phillips (8,200, 35.08%). DFL incumbent Steve Simon was unopposed (27,123 votes, 100%). Jude and Simon advance to the Nov. 3 general election ([MN SOS official results](https://electionresults.sos.mn.gov/Results/Index?ersElectionId=200&scenario=AllwithoutJudicial&electionDate=8%2F11%2F2026%2012%3A00%3A00%20AM), accessed 2026-08-12).

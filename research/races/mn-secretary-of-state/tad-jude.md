## Platform

Running on a promise of "transparency in elections" ([AlphaNews](https://alphanews.org/tad-jude-launches-run-for-secretary-of-state-on-promise-of-transparency-in-elections/)). Cites his experience as a legislator, district court judge, attorney, and small business owner, and says he understands the importance of making it easy for honest businesses to register with the state ([InForum](https://www.inforum.com/news/minnesota/tad-jude-announces-bid-for-minnesota-secretary-of-state)).

## Background & Record

Age 73, of Maple Grove, Minnesota. St. Cloud native. Was the youngest person ever elected to the Minnesota Legislature, winning a State House seat in 1972 at age 20; later served six years in the Minnesota State Senate ([InForum](https://www.inforum.com/news/minnesota/tad-jude-announces-bid-for-minnesota-secretary-of-state)). Served 11 years as a district court judge for Minnesota's 10th Judicial District ([InForum](https://www.inforum.com/news/minnesota/tad-jude-announces-bid-for-minnesota-secretary-of-state)). Also served six years in the Army Security Agency ([InForum](https://www.inforum.com/news/minnesota/tad-jude-announces-bid-for-minnesota-secretary-of-state)). Ran for Minnesota's 3rd Congressional District against Rep. Kelly Morrison in 2024, and ran for Minnesota Attorney General in 2022, losing the Republican nomination to Jim Schultz ([InForum](https://www.inforum.com/news/minnesota/tad-jude-announces-bid-for-minnesota-secretary-of-state)).

## Platform

Positions himself as a political outsider; criticizes state government spending and welfare fraud ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)).

## Background & Record

Businessman and Army veteran; spent roughly 27 years at Fortune 100 healthcare companies (Johnson & Johnson, Roche Labs, Medtronic, Covidien), rising to Global Vice President with P&L responsibility for an $850 million business unit; currently Vice President and Chief Commercial Officer at PotentiaMetrics ([Equilar/LinkedIn bio aggregation](https://people.equilar.com/bio/person/kendall-qualls-triyam/43394083)). Was the 2020 Republican nominee for MN-3 (lost to Dean Phillips by 11 points) and sought the GOP governor endorsement in 2022, losing to Scott Jensen.

## Party Service

Won the 2026 MN GOP endorsement for governor ([Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/2026/05/30/kendall-qualls-wins-gop-endorsement-for-governor/)); prior GOP nominee/candidate in earlier cycles (verify).

## Opposition & Criticism

Demuth argues polling shows she, not Qualls, is best positioned to beat Klobuchar in November ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)).

## Endorsements

Minnesota GOP endorsement, won after 10 ballots at the Duluth convention, defeating Demuth ([Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/2026/05/30/kendall-qualls-wins-gop-endorsement-for-governor/), [Northern News Now](https://northernnewsnow.com/2026/05/30/gop-delegates-weigh-candidates-governor-endorsement/)). Sought President Trump's endorsement, but Trump endorsed rival Mike Lindell instead; Qualls responded that he "didn't take it as a personal slight" ([KAALtv](https://kaaltv.com/news/kendall-qualls-releases-statement-on-president-trumps-endorsement-of-mike-lindell-in-minnesota-gubernatorial-race/)).

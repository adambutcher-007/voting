## Platform

Describes himself as the "values candidate" and a "constitutional conservative" with an "originalist view of the constitution"; has said abortion is the biggest issue hurting Minnesota ([krhin4gov.com](https://krhin4gov.com/about/), [KTTC](https://www.kttc.com/2026/05/14/one-on-one-with-minnesota-gubernatorial-candidate-john-krhin/)).

## Background & Record

Born in Chisholm, Minnesota, the youngest of eight children of Peter Krhin, a naturalized citizen who emigrated from Austria in 1913; now lives north of Hibbing. Married to wife Julia for nearly 53 years, with 8 children and 34 grandchildren ([krhin4gov.com](https://krhin4gov.com/about/)). Listed as a candidate in the Republican primary for governor ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Minnesota_gubernatorial_and_lieutenant_gubernatorial_election,_2026)).

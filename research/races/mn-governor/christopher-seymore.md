## Background & Record

In 1993, founded LCORPS (Life-Changing Out Reach Programs & Services), a nonprofit describing its mission as helping men, women, and youth develop maturity, responsibility, and self-esteem ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Christopher_Seymore_(Minnesota))). Served as a pastor at the Church of God in Christ (2000–2007) and as an executive neighborhood guide with the Boy Scouts of America (2009–2013) ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Christopher_Seymore_(Minnesota))). Listed as a candidate in the DFL primary for governor ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Minnesota_gubernatorial_and_lieutenant_gubernatorial_election,_2026)). No dedicated campaign website was located as of 2026-08-11 — his public presence appears to be a campaign blog (chrisseymoreforminnesotagovernore.blogspot.com) and a Facebook page ("Chris Seymore For Minnesota," @Chris4MN).

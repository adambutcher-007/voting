## Platform

Positions himself as a political outsider; criticizes Demuth as too willing to work with Democrats and faults state spending and welfare fraud ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)).

## Background & Record

Founder and CEO of My Pillow, Inc., the Minnesota-based bedding manufacturer he built after inventing the "MyPillow" product in 2004 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)). Stepped down from day-to-day leadership of My Pillow specifically to run for governor ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/mike-lindell-steps-down-from-mypillow-to-campaign-for-governor-of-minnesota-12284619)). A prominent and prolific promoter of false 2020 election-fraud claims, including funding lawsuits and hosting a widely debunked "Cyber Symposium" (see Detailed Research below).

## Party Service

Longtime Trump ally; seeking President Trump's endorsement in this primary ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)).

### Companies & Management Roles
- **1980s:** Operated small businesses in Carver County, MN, including carpet cleaning, lunch wagons, and bars/restaurants ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2004–2025:** Invented the "MyPillow" product and founded/led My Pillow, Inc. as CEO, growing it into a Minnesota manufacturing company ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2020:** Named his son Darren Lindell as My Pillow's Chief Operating Officer, citing his own political ambitions ([Wikipedia, citing Politico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2025 (ahead of governor run):** Stepped down from day-to-day leadership of My Pillow to campaign full-time ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/mike-lindell-steps-down-from-mypillow-to-campaign-for-governor-of-minnesota-12284619)).
- **2021-04–2025-04:** Founder of FrankSpeech Network, Inc.; renamed Mike Lindell Media Corporation effective 2025-04-23 (OTC ticker changed FSBN → MLMC), operating LindellTV.com (launched 2021-04-19) and Vocl.com (formerly FrankSocial, launched 2022-04) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **Undated:** Financial stake and board connection to Phoenix Biotechnology, a biopharmaceutical company, via fellow board member Andrew Whitney — tied to his 2020 promotion of oleandrin as a COVID-19 treatment ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2019 (founded):** Lindell Foundation, an addiction-recovery nonprofit, and the Lindell Recovery Network ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2020-05:** Became Minnesota campaign chair for Trump's 2020 reelection campaign ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2023-01-27:** Ran for Chair of the Republican National Committee; lost, receiving 4 of 167 votes ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).

### Fraud & Welfare Views
Lindell's public "fraud" focus is almost entirely about alleged election fraud, not welfare fraud; *no information found* on a specific welfare-policy or welfare-fraud statement.
- **2020-11–2021 (ongoing):** Central promoter of false claims that Dominion Voting Systems and Smartmatic conspired to rig the 2020 election ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2021-02-05:** Aired the "Absolute Proof" documentary on OANN ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2021-02-22:** Dominion filed a $1.3 billion defamation suit against Lindell and My Pillow ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2021-04-19:** My Pillow filed a $1.6 billion countersuit against Dominion alleging defamation ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2022-05:** A federal court dismissed Lindell/My Pillow's claims against Dominion and Smartmatic, finding some claims frivolous under Rule 11 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2023-08:** Said he would fly drones with wireless "sniffing" devices near polling places to detect internet-connected voting machines, framed as preventing voting fraud ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2025-06-16:** A federal jury in Colorado found Lindell liable for defaming Eric Coomer, a former Dominion security director, ordering Lindell and FrankSpeech to pay $2.3 million ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).
- **2025-09-26:** A federal judge ruled Lindell had defamed Smartmatic with 51 false claims; damages proceedings ongoing ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Lindell)).

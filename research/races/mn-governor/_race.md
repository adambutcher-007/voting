# Minnesota Governor, 2026

Incumbent Gov. Tim Walz (DFL) initially sought a third term but ended his campaign in January 2026; US Sen. Amy Klobuchar launched a campaign for governor on January 29, 2026 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Minnesota_gubernatorial_election), [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Minnesota_gubernatorial_and_lieutenant_gubernatorial_election,_2026)). DFL primary: Klobuchar, Rick DeVoe, Paul Ference, Kobey Layne, Christopher Seymore. Republican primary (a record seven candidates): Lisa Demuth, Mike Lindell, Kendall Qualls (GOP-endorsed), Loner Blue, Raul Estrada, John Krhin, Ross Nova ([Ballotpedia News](https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/06/11/record-high-seven-candidates-running-in-minnesotas-republican-gubernatorial-primary/)). ## Primary Results (Aug. 11, 2026)

DFL: Amy Klobuchar won with 26,887 votes (88.94%), far ahead of Kobey Layne (1,540, 5.09%) and other DFL filers (Christopher Seymore and Rick DeVoe did not appear as separate lines in the MN SOS results table). Republican: House Speaker Lisa Demuth won with 11,268 votes (44.74%), ahead of Mike Lindell (8,200, 32.56%) and party-endorsed Kendall Qualls (5,183, 20.58%); John Krhin, Ross Nova, Raul Estrada, and Loner Blue each received under 1%. Klobuchar and Demuth advance to the Nov. 3 general election, setting up Minnesota's first all-woman gubernatorial race ([MN SOS official results](https://electionresults.sos.mn.gov/Results/Index?ersElectionId=200&scenario=AllwithoutJudicial&electionDate=8%2F11%2F2026%2012%3A00%3A00%20AM), accessed 2026-08-12).

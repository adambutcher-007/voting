## Background & Record

US Senator from Minnesota since 2007; former Hennepin County Attorney; 2020 presidential candidate; has dominated her Senate races ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/amy-klobuchar-dominated-her-senate-races-will-she-do-the-same-in-governors-race/601651767)).

## Party Service

Longtime statewide DFL standard-bearer and top statewide vote-getter across multiple cycles ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/amy-klobuchar-dominated-her-senate-races-will-she-do-the-same-in-governors-race/601651767)). Far out-raising GOP rivals ([Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/briefs/klobuchar-continues-to-far-out-raise-gop-candidates-for-minnesota-governor/)).

## Opposition & Criticism

Republican attacks include slipping rural support, alignment with Walz and Biden, and past reports about treatment of her staff ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/minnesota-poll-klobuchar-leads-all-gop-candidates-for-governor/601850018)). Demuth's campaign launched WalzThirdTerm.com claiming Klobuchar "stood shoulder-to-shoulder with Tim Walz" ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)); Qualls calls this her "first real test," and both question her engagement in the race ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)).

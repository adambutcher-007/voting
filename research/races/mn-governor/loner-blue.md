## Background & Record

Retiree, born May 9, 1956 in Park Rapids, Minnesota, now living in Minneapolis ([VoteSmart](https://justfacts.votesmart.org/candidate/218179/loner-blue)). A perennial statewide candidate: sought the Republican nomination for governor in 2022 and ran for US Senate in 2024, losing the Republican primary that August ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Loner_Blue), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_United_States_Senate_election_in_Minnesota)). Running for governor again in 2026 with running mate Andrew Maass ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Loner_Blue)).

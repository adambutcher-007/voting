## Platform

Argues she is the Republican best positioned to beat Klobuchar in November, citing polling ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)). Attacks Klobuchar as a continuation of the Walz administration via WalzThirdTerm.com ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)).

## Background & Record

Speaker of the Minnesota House ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)).

## Party Service

Led House Republicans to the speakership; stayed in the race after losing the GOP endorsement to Qualls ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/lisa-demuth-continues-governor-race/), [Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/2026/05/30/kendall-qualls-wins-gop-endorsement-for-governor/)).

## Opposition & Criticism

Rivals Lindell and Qualls claim she is too willing to work with Democrats and failed to cut government spending and welfare fraud ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)). Running against the endorsed candidate may draw party-loyalist criticism ([Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/briefs/lisa-demuth-to-run-in-gop-primary-for-governor-setting-up-three-way-election-in-august/)).

## Endorsements

Lost the MN GOP endorsement to Qualls ([Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/2026/05/30/kendall-qualls-wins-gop-endorsement-for-governor/)). Sought President Trump's endorsement, saying she "would definitely welcome" it, but Trump ultimately endorsed rival Mike Lindell instead ([MinnPost](https://minnpost.com/state-government/2025/11/house-speaker-lisa-demuth-seeks-trumps-endorsement-in-bid-to-unseat-gov-tim-walz/), [Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/demuth-wont-change-her-style-even-as-her-path-narrows-for-the-gop-governor-nomination/601873295)). Endorsed by the NRA and the Minnesota Gun Owners Caucus/PAC ([gunowners.mn](https://gunowners.mn/press-release-lisa-demuth-endorsed/)).

## Campaign Finance

Trails Klobuchar in fundraising ([Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/briefs/klobuchar-continues-to-far-out-raise-gop-candidates-for-minnesota-governor/)). See [MN Campaign Finance Board](https://cfb.mn.gov/).

## News & Notes

One poll shows she performs best against Klobuchar among GOP candidates ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)). Seeking President Trump's endorsement along with Qualls and Lindell ([CBS Minnesota](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/demuth-qualls-minnesota-primary-governor-election/)).

**Updated 2026-08-07:** Debated Klobuchar, Qualls, and Lindell at Farmfest on Aug. 5, making her pitch to farmers and ag-industry professionals ([Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/2026/08/05/minnesotas-candidates-for-governor-make-their-case-to-farmers-ag-industry-pros/)).

## Detailed Research

### Bills Authored or Co-Authored
- **Undated (MN House term):** Chief-authored an early bill targeting daycare fraud ([AlphaNews](https://alphanews.org/karin-miller-a-window-into-the-leadership-of-lisa-demuth/)).
- **Undated (MN House term):** Championed a "Heartbeat Bill" restricting abortion ([AlphaNews](https://alphanews.org/karin-miller-a-window-into-the-leadership-of-lisa-demuth/)).
- **2026:** Chief author of House File 1, establishing a centralized state Office of Inspector General to consolidate agency fraud-investigation functions, require payment holds on suspected fraud, and create a fraud-reporting hotline ([KSTP](https://kstp.com/kstp-news/top-news/house-dfl-rolls-out-nearly-a-dozen-bills-as-part-of-anti-fraud-package/)). Full itemized bill list available via her Minnesota House member profile.

### Companies & Management Roles
- **Undated:** Manages commercial properties ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/who-is-republican-lisa-demuth-minnesotas-first-house-speaker-of-color/601218089)). (Note: her husband Nick Demuth, not Lisa, is president of Avon Plastics in Paynesville — some coverage conflates the two; treated separately here.)

### Political Offices Held
- **2007–2018:** Rockville-Cold Spring-Richmond (ROCORI) School District school board member, first elected as a write-in in 2007 and reelected twice ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Demuth)).
- **2019-01-08–present:** Minnesota House of Representatives, District 13A, first elected November 2018 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Demuth)).
- **2021–2022:** Assistant Minority Leader, MN House ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Demuth)).
- **2022–2025:** House Minority Leader ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Demuth)).
- **2025-02-06–present:** Speaker of the Minnesota House — first Black person and first female Republican to hold the role ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Demuth)).
- **2025-11-02:** Announced campaign for Governor of Minnesota ([Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/2025/11/02/gop-minnesota-house-speaker-lisa-demuth-launches-campaign-for-governor/)).

- **2026-05-30:** Spoke at the Minnesota GOP state convention in Duluth ([Minnesota Reformer](https://minnesotareformer.com/briefs/lisa-demuth-to-run-in-gop-primary-for-governor-setting-up-three-way-election-in-august/)).

I will continue to protect our Second Amendment rights." ([KARE 11](https://kare11.com/article/news/politics/assault-weapons-ban-gun-package-unlikely-vote-tied-minnesota-house/89-e082285c-0387-420d-9771-b9a4ef94199a)).

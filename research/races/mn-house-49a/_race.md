# Minnesota House District 49A, 2026

District 49A covers south Minnetonka and Eden Prairie north of Highway 5 ([EP Local News](https://www.eplocalnews.org/2022/03/02/political-redistricting-for-ep-deja-vu-all-over-again/)). Incumbent DFL Rep. Alex Falconer is seeking re-election against GOP-endorsed Jeff Houdek ([EP Local News](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/06/24/2026-ballots-take-shape-for-eden-prairie-voters/), [Houdek endorsement](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/06/18/houdek-endorsed-by-republicans-in-house-49a-race/)). Note: whether the voter is in 49A or 49B depends on their address (Highway 5 boundary).

## Platform

Says his goals are "restoring public safety in Hennepin County, bringing back common sense to criminal justice, and protecting Hennepin County's most vulnerable residents" ([FOX 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/whos-running-hennepin-county-attorney-2026-election-list)). At the LWV forum, criticized a lack of collaboration with other law-enforcement agencies ([CCX Media](https://ccxmedia.org/news/five-hennepin-county-attorney-candidates-make-case-to-voters-at-lwv-forum/)).

## Background & Record

Former Acting US Attorney for Minnesota; partner at Jones Day; adjunct professor at St. Thomas School of Law; former US Marine; senior DOJ official under President Biden ([FOX 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/whos-running-hennepin-county-attorney-2026-election-list), [Minnesota Lawyer](https://minnlawyer.com/2025/11/13/hennepin-county-attorney-race-2026/)).

## Party Service

Nonpartisan race; served in the Biden DOJ ([FOX 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/whos-running-hennepin-county-attorney-2026-election-list)).

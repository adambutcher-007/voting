# Hennepin County Attorney, 2026

Open seat: incumbent Mary Moriarty is not seeking a second term ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Hennepin_County_Attorney_election), [MinnPost](https://www.minnpost.com/metro/2026/06/with-moriarty-bowing-out-as-hennepin-county-attorney-the-race-is-on-for-who-will-replace-her-and-how/)). Five candidates filed: Anders Folk, Cedrick Frazier (DFL-endorsed), Diane M. Krenz, Hao Nguyen, Matt Pelikan ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Hennepin_County_Attorney_election), [Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/hennepin-county-attorney-race-cedrick-frazier-dfl-endorsement/601856726)). Nonpartisan office; primary narrows the field to two. Candidates debated at a League of Women Voters forum ([CCX Media](https://ccxmedia.org/news/five-hennepin-county-attorney-candidates-make-case-to-voters-at-lwv-forum/), [EP Local News](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/06/29/five-candidates-make-their-case-to-replace-moriarty/)).

## Primary Results (Aug. 11, 2026)

This nonpartisan blanket primary sent the top two vote-getters to the general election: Cedrick Frazier (87,447 votes) and Anders Folk (55,610). Diane Krenz (46,150), Hao Nguyen (36,424), and Matt Pelikan (17,648) were eliminated. Frazier and Folk face off Nov. 3 to succeed outgoing County Attorney Mary Moriarty ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/cedrick-frazier-wins-hennepin-county-attorney-primary-advances-to-face-anders-folk/601875120), accessed 2026-08-12).

## Platform

Raises concerns about the office's liberal use of plea bargains ([CCX Media](https://ccxmedia.org/news/five-hennepin-county-attorney-candidates-make-case-to-voters-at-lwv-forum/)). Stresses a case-by-case approach rooted in both law and human judgment, arguing against sweeping prosecution policies driven solely by statistics ([EP Local News](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/24/hao-nguyen-for-hennepin-county-attorney/)).

## Background & Record

Former assistant Ramsey County Attorney with 15 years of prosecution experience ([FOX 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/whos-running-hennepin-county-attorney-2026-election-list)). Currently a senior-level manager in a metro prosecutor's office overseeing more than 300 employees ([FOX 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/whos-running-hennepin-county-attorney-2026-election-list)). Before practicing law, served as a corrections officer, police officer, and sheriff's deputy ([FOX 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/whos-running-hennepin-county-attorney-2026-election-list)). Arrived in the United States as a child refugee who fled Vietnam with his single mother and brothers ([FOX 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/whos-running-hennepin-county-attorney-2026-election-list)).

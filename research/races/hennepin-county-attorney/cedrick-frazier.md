## Background & Record

DFL state representative serving New Hope and Crystal since 2020; vice chair of the Public Safety and Judiciary committees; began his legal career as a Hennepin County public defender; former legal counsel for Minneapolis Public Schools and labor attorney for Minnesota educators ([FOX 9](https://www.fox9.com/news/whos-running-hennepin-county-attorney-2026-election-list), [Minneapolis Times](https://minneapolistimes.com/tracking-the-2026-hennepin-county-attorney-race/)).

## Party Service

Sitting DFL legislator; won the Hennepin County DFL endorsement for this race ([Star Tribune](https://www.startribune.com/hennepin-county-attorney-race-cedrick-frazier-dfl-endorsement/601856726)).

## Opposition & Criticism

Rivals at the LWV forum emphasized prosecutorial experience he lacks relative to career prosecutors in the field ([CCX Media](https://ccxmedia.org/news/five-hennepin-county-attorney-candidates-make-case-to-voters-at-lwv-forum/), [MinnPost](https://www.minnpost.com/metro/2026/06/with-moriarty-bowing-out-as-hennepin-county-attorney-the-race-is-on-for-who-will-replace-her-and-how/)).

# Eden Prairie School Board (ISD 272), 2026

Four school board seats, each carrying a four-year term, are on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). **Six candidates filed** before the 5 p.m. July 28, 2026 deadline: incumbents Aaron Casper and Abby Libsack, plus Madalynn Gerold, Kellie Stecher, Mariana "Mari" Verdugo and Laura Wall ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)).

Casper was first elected in 2019 and reelected in 2022; Libsack was first elected in 2022 ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). Incumbents Steve Bartz and Debjyoti "DD" Dwivedy did not file for reelection; Bartz joined the board after a 2021 special election and was elected again in 2022, and Dwivedy was elected in 2022 ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). All four terms end in January 2027 ([EP Local News, 2026-07-29](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)).

**Field is now final:** no candidates withdrew by the 5 p.m. July 30, 2026 deadline ([EP Local News](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/07/29/seven-file-for-eden-prairie-city-council-six-for-school-board/)). The race is nonpartisan. Most Eden Prairie precincts are in the Eden Prairie school district, though a few fall within the Minnetonka and Hopkins districts, so some city voters will see a different school board race or none at all ([EP Local News, 2026-04-17](https://eplocalnews.org/2026/04/17/what-to-know-about-eden-prairies-2026-elections/)).
